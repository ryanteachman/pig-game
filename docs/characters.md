# Character Visual Notes

Written from the reference photos in `assets/references/` (which are never shipped).
Each entry lists colors, silhouette, and the 2–3 signature features that must survive at pixel scale.

## Mr. Pig (16×16, player)
- **Colors:** soft fuzzy pink body (light pink highlights, dusty-rose shading), deeper pink nostrils, tiny black bead eyes, **royal "Duke" blue T-shirt** with white lettering.
- **Silhouette:** a big round head that is most of the sprite, two floppy triangular ears pointing up and out, a short stubby body in a shirt, and four stubby pink legs.
- **Signature features:**
  1. The **blue shirt with a white letter mark** on the chest. He wears it in every photo.
  2. A **large, round, lighter-pink snout** with two dark-pink nostrils, set low on the face.
  3. **Small black bead eyes** set wide apart, and floppy outward ears. Cheeky look.

## Mr. Big Pig (32×32, rival #1)
- **Colors:** very pale baby-pink (almost peach) smooth plush, pink inner ears, brown button eyes, pink snout tip with two tiny dark nostrils.
- **Silhouette:** a **huge, round, squishy oval blob**, wider than it is tall, with no visible neck. Two small rounded ears sit on top like flaps.
- **Signature features:**
  1. The **giant round blob body** (2× Mr. Pig's size).
  2. **Small brown dot eyes** spaced far apart, low on the face.
  3. A **short cylindrical snout** poking out at the bottom-center, with a pink tip.
- In-game he wears a straw farmer hat (used in the Scarecrow Swap prank).

## Sir Oinks A Lot (16×16, rival #2)
- **Colors:** two-tone plush: **pink top half and cream/white belly half**, flat pink folded ears, brown dot eyes, and a peach-pink snout with dark nostrils.
- **Silhouette:** a round, loaf-shaped oval pig (like a smaller Big Pig) with the two-tone split. In-game he's a knight, so he wears a **silver helmet with a red plume** and a silver breastplate band.
- **Signature features:**
  1. The **pink-over-cream two-tone body**.
  2. **Flat, folded-forward ears** and brown dot eyes on either side of a big snout.
  3. **Knight armor:** a helmet with a red plume (turned pink after the Pink Armor prank).

## The Shleepy Stone (16×16, companion, "she")
- **Colors:** sunny **mustard yellow** painted stone, **black painted hair**, dark grey-black eye lines and mouth.
- **Silhouette:** a **half-dome**: round top and a flatter bottom, like a smooth river stone.
- **Signature features:**
  1. **Black "hair" cap** over the top third, with the yellow forehead rising into a little **peak at the center**.
  2. **Closed, sleepy eyes** drawn as two short horizontal lines.
  3. A small **round "o" mouth**, as if mid-snore.
- Awake frame: eyes become small half-open dots. Asleep frame: lines plus floating "z" particles.

## Annarose (16×24, final judge)
- **Colors:** dark, almost black hair; warm light skin; **light-blue pajamas with tiny white snowflake/print dots** (she also wears a cozy navy hoodie); thin dark glasses frames; small silver hoop earrings.
- **Silhouette:** a person a head taller than the pigs. Shoulder-length dark hair pulled back with side-swept bangs, a loose comfy pajama top, and long pajama pants.
- **Signature features:**
  1. **Dark hair** framing the face, with side-swept bangs.
  2. **Glasses** (two dark rectangles across the eyes) and a playful smile.
  3. **Light-blue patterned pajamas.** Bonus detail: she **loves cheese**, which is why cheese is the collectible.
